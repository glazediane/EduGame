Unlock Request

Request an Unlock Token

Your account has been temporarily locked after repeated failed login attempts. The system automatically generates an unlock token for your account, and you must bring that token to the ISAO office.

Important: The ISAO does not generate the token. The system does. The ISAO will only provide the token to the admin for account release.
  • 1. After lockout, the system will display your generated unlock token.
  • 2. Bring your valid ID and the unlock token to the ISAO office.
  • 3. The ISAO staff will give the token to the admin for manual verification and unlock.
  • 4. After the admin unlocks and logs the request, you may log in again.
